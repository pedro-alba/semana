## 2. What data we process

| Data | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- |
| E-mail | You provide it when you sign up. |
| Password | You provide it when you sign up. We store only an irreversibly encrypted version; no one, not even us, can read it. |
| Time zone | Read automatically from your device when you sign up, to know when your week starts. |
| Content you write | Projects, tasks, subtasks, notes, links and the task log. |
| Access data | IP address, date and time of logins and login attempts, and the session cookie. |

We do not ask for your name, CPF, phone number, location or sensitive personal data. If you write data of that kind in your notes, it is processed only to store it and show it to you.

## 3. What we use it for and on which legal basis

| Purpose | Data | Legal basis (LGPD, art. 7) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Create and keep your account, store your content and show it on any device | E-mail, password, time zone, content | Performance of a contract (item V): the Terms of Use |
| Recover your password | E-mail | Performance of a contract (item V) |
| Protect your account against unauthorized access, such as blocking repeated login attempts | IP, date and time of attempts | Legitimate interest (item IX) |
| Keep access logs for the period required by law | IP, date and time of access | Compliance with a legal obligation (item II), Marco Civil da Internet, art. 15 [CONFIRM WHETHER IT APPLIES TO YOUR CASE] |

We do not use your data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not use it to train artificial intelligence systems. No one on our team reads your content, except when you ask for help and allow it, or when there is a court order.

## 4. Cookies and storage on your device

- We use **a single cookie**, an essential one, that keeps you logged in. It expires after 30 days without use or when you log out. We do not use analytics, advertising or third-party cookies.
- To work without internet, Semana keeps in your device's browser a copy of what you have already opened and the changes that have not been sent yet. When you log out, that copy is erased from that device.

## 8. How we protect it

Connection always encrypted (HTTPS), passwords stored irreversibly, isolation between accounts verified by automated tests, cleanup of potentially dangerous content, and daily backups. No system is fully immune to failures. If there is an incident that may bring you relevant risk or harm, we will notify you and the National Data Protection Authority (Autoridade Nacional de Proteção de Dados, ANPD), as the law requires.

## 9. Your rights

Under art. 18 of the LGPD, you can ask for:

- confirmation that we process your data, and access to it;
- correction of incomplete, inaccurate or outdated data;
- anonymization, blocking or deletion of unnecessary data or data processed in breach of the law;
- portability of your data;
- information about who we share your data with;
- review of decisions made solely by automated means (Semana makes no decisions of that kind).

You can delete your account at any time from the profile menu, which erases all your data. For other requests, write to **[PRIVACY E-MAIL]**. We will reply within [15] days. You can also file a complaint with the [ANPD](https://www.gov.br/anpd).
